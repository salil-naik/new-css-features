CSS Forms · Baseline 2026

Content-Sized Form Controls

The field-sizing: content property lets inputs, textareas, and selects size themselves to fit their content. Edit the controls to compare fixed widths with content-based sizing.

Fixed size

Each control fills the available width, even when its content is short.

Size to content

With field-sizing: content, controls grow or shrink as their content changes.

Browsers without field-sizing support keep the usual control sizing.
